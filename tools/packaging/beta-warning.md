> [!WARNING]
> **karasu-slicer is beta software: use it with care.** It has been tested on few printers so
> far, and a defect in the files it writes can make a print fail: a part mirrored or turned,
> layers exposed too long or too short, a model that comes loose from the plate. A failed print
> can leave cured resin in the vat; when the next print presses on it, it can tear the release
> film or crack the printer's screen.
>
> - On a printer whose image orientation is not verified (see the
>   [supported printers](https://github.com/dazeroit/karasu-slicer#supported-printers)), print a
>   small test part first.
> - Watch the first layers of a print, and stop the printer if something looks wrong.
> - After a failed print, check the vat and its film for cured pieces before printing again.
> - Check the exposure, lift and layer settings against the recommendations of the printer's
>   and the resin's makers.
>
