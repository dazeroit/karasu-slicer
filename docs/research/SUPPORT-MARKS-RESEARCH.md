# Support marks: the venom arm, the tip test and Lychee's supports (research, 2026-11-18)

**Purpose:** find what decides how many marks the supports leave on a printed part and what kind,
after the owner's print of the venom arm (E-SUP-05). Earlier evidence, about whether supports
hold, is in [SUPPORT-PRESETS-RESEARCH.md](SUPPORT-PRESETS-RESEARCH.md) (E-SUP-01 to E-SUP-04);
the numbering continues here. This record is research only. It changes no value, and it sets no
default.

**Method:**
- prints made by the owner, with the owner's photos and reports;
- karasu-slicer's own files;
- files that Lychee Slicer exported from a specimen made for the purpose. Only the files were
  read, by independent scripts and by UVtools used as a program. Nothing of either application
  was examined.

Statements are marked **observed** (seen in a print, a photo or a file, or measured in a file),
**inferred** (deduced from observations) or **hypothesis** (to be tested). Raw evidence (photos,
projects, exported files) is kept outside the repository and cited by path and SHA-256.

## The owner's requirement (2026-11-18)

- Supports should leave as few marks as possible.
- Where a support leaves a mark, it should stand proud of the surface, so it can be sanded away,
  never a pit, which would need filling.
- The owner wanted to try ball contacts as wide as the contact itself, a rounded end like the
  ones Lychee's supports have.

## The venom arm (E-SUP-05, owner, printed overnight 2026-11-17)

**Conditions (observed in the project `arm_r_v2.karasu` and the printed file
`venom_arm_r_v2_2_karasu.pp1`, SHA-256
`c247dea2d523afe70e497450d4bde71eacfa8f53a88cf10394667ddac6ebae91`):**
- Photon P1, the owner's "Normal 0.05 mm": 2.0 s per layer; 5 bottom layers at 25 s; 10
  transition layers; anti-aliasing 16 on the model, supports binary. The model is hollowed and
  lifted 5 mm. The file has 2,806 layers.
- 1,510 supports: 1,501 automatic ones and 9 placed by hand.
  - All but one are Medium after TD-136: a cone contact 0.5 mm wide, sunk 0.1 mm, on a tip and
    shaft 1.0 mm wide.
  - Bases: 285 on the plate, 1,020 on other supports, 205 on the model.
- 1,118 braces.
- A web raft (TD-144): 0.5 mm thick, with fillets 2.0 mm wide and 0.8 mm tall around the shafts.

**Outcome (owner, 13 photos in `E:\3dprint\karasu_slicer_compare_test\venom\arm_r\foto\`):**
- The arm printed whole, fingers and claws included, and the supports came off.
- Many small dots stand proud where the contacts were, across the palm and the forearm. They
  can be sanded.
- The raft looked thicker than the 0.5 mm set.

## The tip test (E-SUP-06, owner, printed 2026-11-18)

**The specimen (observed, generated by a script outside the repository):**
- A tile 30 × 24 × 2 mm. Under it, a half-cylinder of radius 6 mm lies along the tile's 30 mm
  side, flat face up, against the middle 12 mm of the tile. The half circle is made of 48
  segments.
- The underside therefore offers three kinds of surface:
  - two flat bands, 6 mm wide, facing straight down;
  - the cylinder's curve, with every slope from 0 to 90°;
  - two concave right-angle corners, where each band meets the cylinder's side.
- One to four marker dots on top (cylinders 2 mm wide, standing 1 mm proud) tell the specimens
  apart.

**Conditions (observed in `tip_test.karasu`, SHA-256
`8e6a75c334abc92181084e3a3949365212ca7fd4022c8645ba13f90e618fb56c`, and `tip_test.pp1`, SHA-256

at 45bc195):**
- **Plate:** specimens A, B and C side by side, each lifted 5 mm, on the arm's web raft and
  print setting. The file has 280 layers and an estimated 2,726 s.
- **Supports:** each specimen has the same 161 automatic supports, planned once by
  `karasu-cli slice --auto-supports --auto-minis off` (Medium, branches on):
  - 69 stand on the plate and 92 on other supports;
  - 120 are on the flat bands and 41 on the cylinder.
- **Contact:** only the contact differs between the specimens:

| Specimen | Contact | Narrowest width at the surface | Section in the last layer below a band (measured) | Section 1 mm below |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | cone 0.5 mm, sunk 0.1 mm | 0.52 mm (`d + (body − d) · depth / length`) | 0.54 mm | 0.78 mm |
| B | ball 0.5 mm, sunk 0.1 mm | 0.40 mm (`2√(p(D − p))`) | 0.44 mm | 0.75 mm |
| C | ball 0.5 mm, sunk 0.05 mm | 0.30 mm | 0.38 mm | 0.74 mm |

The measured sections are the equivalent diameters of the 120 band tips' areas in the PP1's
layers 219 and 200, the band's underside being at layer 220.

**Read before printing (observed, UVtools 2.2.0 issue detection):**
- **Islands:** at the cylinder's lowest line (layer 100), 75,000 pixels each on B and C, none
  on A.
- **Overhang:** under the bands (layer 220), A 290,000 pixels in all, B 363,000, C 407,000.

As on the earlier test plates (E-SUP-02), UVtools' check compares the lit area below a layer
with that layer's area (inferred from its reports). The thinner necks lower that ratio; they do
not leave a part unsupported.

**Outcome (owner, 2026-11-18; three photos in
`E:\3dprint\karasu_slicer_compare_test\tip_test\foto\`, A
`04d66171df20c7f5b72442a18f2c7b30328be651ad28cc15b9ad0934d1d88f15`, B
`0579bb224e6ffac86b4fccb00e81aea12b1c6a3ef611792d37513fc49e92cf53`, C
`7c33b4cf444eeb48ab6728f9f58f47e9318fce1200b95d278ff28a95288accca`):**
- **Print (observed):** all three printed whole, C's lowest line included. No pit is seen in the
  photos, but they resolve about 50 pixels per mm and are soft, so the dots' heights cannot be
  compared in them.
- **Comparison (owner, in hand):** C leaves slightly better marks than A and B; the difference
  is really small.
- **Largest marks (observed in the photos):** on all three specimens they run along the two
  concave corners. They are about 1 mm wide, twice the contact, and several of A's look broken.
- **Where they come from (measured in the PP1):** those marks are where the row of tips nearest the
  cylinder stands, 14 tips per corner and 0.4 mm from the cylinder's side.
  - In layers 200 to 219, the last millimetre before the band, those tips' sections stand only
    4 to 6 pixels (0.10 to 0.15 mm) from the cylinder's section.
  - The tip widens below the contact while the cylinder's side curves away under the band,
    which is why the gap stays that narrow.

**Findings (inferred):**
1. **Narrowing the neck from 0.52 to 0.30 mm helped only a little.** The contact's shape alone
   is not what decides how many marks are left, or how large they are.
2. **The corner row comes from karasu's outline rule (TD-136).** An underside's outline counts as
   an edge where the surface beyond it stands more than 0.5 mm higher **or lower**
   (`crates/karasu-project/src/support_plan/auto/edges.rs`). At the corner, the surface beyond
   (the cylinder) is lower: the band meets a wall that prints before it, so the band is held
   there by material, not left hanging. It still takes edge tips 0.4 mm in from the corner, as a
   free edge would.
3. **Hypothesis:** a gap of 0.10 to 0.15 mm fills with cured resin and joins those tips to the
   cylinder's side. A joined tip breaks in its 1.0 mm body rather than at its neck, which would
   fit marks about 1 mm wide. The owner has not yet checked it on the specimens.

## Lychee's automatic supports on the specimen (E-SUP-07, owner, 2026-11-18)

**Conditions:**
- **Settings (observed, owner's screenshot of the support panel):**
  - automatic supports, version "Classic", density "Normal";
  - preset Medium at its defaults: a cone tip with a tip diameter of 0.30 mm, a tip length of
    2.50 mm and a diameter of 1.10 mm;
  - mini supports off, Auto-Bracing off, Auto-Parent on, Auto-Lift off.
- **Specimen:** specimen L, in the same orientation, lifted 5 mm, for the Photon P1.
- **Exports:**
  - `tip_test_Lychee.pp1` (SHA-256
    `43caa886a584a8d84069ad50bcb6bc96d4f21e21985c999e3f67969ec3d7b6d7`; the file names
    "Lychee Slicer 8.0.0"; 280 layers; 2,129 s estimated);
  - `tip_test_Lychee.stl`, the supported model (SHA-256
    `b6df89d90cf5d0f584ccdce3e1f674cab58e6485ef5ccd25d6a0a143965bfa0c`; 16,608 triangles).
- **Same settings in both PP1s (observed):** layer height, bottom and transition layer counts,
  exposures and waits are identical to karasu's file.
- **Differences:**
  - lifts: Lychee's are 3 + 3 mm, with a first retract at 1 mm/s; karasu's are 4 + 5 mm. They
    change the time, not the marks;
  - the model's anti-aliasing: the header says 4 in Lychee's file; karasu's print setting
    applies 16;
  - supports print binary in both files.
- Not printed yet.

**The tip (observed, from the STL; the same as E-SUP-04's Medium):**
- An octagonal cone runs from a joint ball as wide as the shaft (1.09 mm) to 0.30 mm. It ends in
  a near-hemispherical cap, 3.1 to 3.2 mm from the ball.
- The cap's deepest point lies 0.150 to 0.153 mm inside the model, about the cap's radius, so
  the neck at the surface is the full 0.30 mm.
- The tips' axes follow the surface's normal to within about 8°.
- Measured in the PP1, the sections below a band are 0.29 mm at layer 219, 0.31 at 218, 0.43 at
  210 and 0.58 at 200.

**The placement (observed, from the STL and the PP1):**

| | Lychee, Medium | karasu, Medium (E-SUP-06) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tips | 44 | 161 |
| On the flat bands (360 mm²) | 20, about 1 per 18 mm² | 120, 1 per 3 mm² |
| Band tips within 1 mm of the cylinder's side | none; the nearest is 3.1 mm away | 28, at 0.4 mm |
| Band tips nearest the outer edges | 0.7 to 1.7 mm in; 0.7 to 0.9 mm from the tile's ends | 0.4 mm in |
| Contacts' area at the surface on the bands | 1.3 mm², 0.35 % | A 25.5 mm² (7.1 %), B 15.1 (4.2 %), C 8.5 (2.4 %) |
| On the cylinder | 24: 10 on the lowest line, 14 at slopes of 8° to 56° | 41: 13 on the lowest line, 28 at slopes of 14° to 39° |
| Shafts on the raft | 32, 1.1 mm; 12 tips hang from them on arms | 69, 1.0 mm; 92 supports stand on others |
| Section 1 mm below a band | 0.58 mm | 0.74 to 0.78 mm |

The band tips stand in two staggered rows per band, 1.8 to 5.4 mm apart along the tile.

**The raft (observed, in the PP1's layers):**
- **Lychee:** a 0.5 mm slab (layers 0 to 9), 0.5 mm beyond the tile. Its sides slope
  outwards going up: the first layer is 0.45 mm narrower on each side than the tenth. The
  shafts rise straight from the slab with a flare under 0.25 mm.
- **karasu:** the web raft is 0.5 mm thick too. At this foot spacing it covers 93 % of the
  footprint plus margin, so it is almost a slab; its sides slope inwards going up (45°). Above
  it, every shaft's fillet narrows from about 1.75 mm at the raft's top to the shaft's 1.0 mm,
  0.8 mm higher, as the fillet is specified (`docs/SUPPORTS-SPEC.md`). The base therefore
  reaches 1.3 mm above the plate before the shafts.

**Read before printing (observed, UVtools 2.2.0 issue detection on Lychee's file):**
- **Island:** the cylinder's whole lowest line at layer 100, 96,444 pixels. karasu's B and C
  showed 75,000 pixels each, A none.
- **Overhang:** both bands entire at layer 220, 663,140 pixels. karasu's A showed 290,000.

**karasu with lighter settings (observed, the same command on specimen A):**
- With `--auto-density 50`, the plan has 89 supports: 70 on the bands (24 of them within 1 mm
  of the cylinder's side) and 19 on the cylinder.
- With `--support-weight light`, it still has 161 supports: the weight changes only their
  sizes.

**Findings (inferred, before Lychee's specimen is printed):**
1. **The tip is not where the two slicers differ most.** Lychee's Medium neck at the surface
   (0.30 mm) is specimen C's. Whatever difference its print shows in the marks will come from
   the number and placement of the tips.
2. **Lychee places a quarter of karasu's tips, and a sixth on flat undersides.** It places none
   near the concave corners and keeps its outer tips 0.7 mm or more from the edges. Even at half
   density, karasu keeps 3.5 times Lychee's tips on the bands, and keeps the corner rows.
3. **The base, not the raft, is likely what looked thick in E-SUP-05.** The raft itself is
   0.5 mm in both slicers. What karasu adds are the 0.8 mm fillets, standing on top of the raft.
4. **Whether Lychee's sparse tips hold is not known.** Its band contacts cover 0.35 % of the
   bands, where the least that held on karasu's tiles (E-SUP-03) was 2.1 % with edge tips. But
   the bands are joined to the cylinder, which prints first and holds them along one side, unlike
   those free tiles. UVtools flags Lychee's file more than karasu's. Its print will show whether
   the bands come out flat and the cylinder's lowest line whole.

## The pixel's height on the Photon P1 (observed, 2026-11-18)

- **Observed:** the two PP1s rasterise the specimen's 24 mm side (along the plate's Y)
  differently:
  - Lychee's file uses 968 pixels, that is 24.8 µm each, though its header gives the panel as
    126.48 mm, which is 24.703 µm for 5120 pixels;
  - karasu's uses 976 pixels, 24.605 µm each, from Photon Workshop's area (125.976 mm).
  The 30 mm side (along X, 16.8 µm) is 1786 pixels in both.
- **Inferred:** one of the two prints its Y sizes about 0.8 % off: 0.19 mm on 24 mm, 1 mm on
  126 mm.
- **Background:** the pitch is already an open question:
  - [PRINTER-PROFILE-SOURCES-RESEARCH.md](PRINTER-PROFILE-SOURCES-RESEARCH.md), "Photon P1 files
    from four slicers";
  - [CATALOG-SOURCES.md](../CATALOG-SOURCES.md), "Known limits".
- **Proposed check:** measure, with calipers, both sides of a printed tile from each slicer.
  Overcure adds about the same to both sides, so compare how far each side exceeds its size. If
  the 24 mm side of karasu's tile exceeds its size by about 0.2 mm more than the 30 mm side
  does, the panel's pitch is 24.8 µm; if both exceed it by the same, 24.605 µm is right.

## Open questions and candidate changes

**Waiting for the owner:**
- the print of Lychee's specimen, with the same photos and checks as E-SUP-06;
- whether the corner marks of E-SUP-06 are joined to the cylinder's side (hypothesis 3);
- the calipers check of the P1's pixel height.

**Candidate changes, not decided:** each needs its own design, a decision the owner approves,
and a test print.
1. **Outline tips only at free edges.** Give edge tips only where the surface beyond an outline
   is missing or higher, not where it is lower (a concave corner whose wall holds the
   underside), or keep tips clear of a wall by more than the gap that cures shut.
2. **Fewer tips on flat undersides in Medium,** if Lychee's specimen shows that far fewer hold.
3. **Lower fillets, or none, on a raft:** the fillets bring the base from 0.5 to 1.3 mm.
4. **A slender tip:** a narrower body under the neck, as Lychee's 0.58 mm at 1 mm below against
   karasu's 0.74 to 0.78 mm.
