with every value as published.

  Photon P1's Standard Resin is Photon Workshop's default (A11), with 15 transition layers,
  anti-aliasing 16 and NFEP film.

  printer's bottom layers from its manual (E3). Ranges give their middle, which makes the

**karasu-slicer's own choices (not manufacturer values):**

- Machine limits (layer height 0.01–0.2 mm, exposure up to 300 s, speed up to 20 mm/s,
  lift up to 20 mm) are validation bounds.
- Transition layers are 0 and anti-aliasing off where the manufacturers' tables give
  none.
- The tilting ELEGOO printers (Saturn 4 Ultra 16K, Saturn 4 Ultra, Mars 5 Ultra) have no
  Z motion in ELEGOO's table: their settings carry a 5 mm lift at 3 mm/s, karasu-slicer's
  value, and are starting points. Their bottom layers come from the manuals (E3; the
  16K's manual of 2026-09-03 gives the 5 layers the catalog already used).
- Anti-aliasing 1 (none) where Anycubic's table gives none ("/") or cannot be taken as
  printed (the Mono X's row repeats the Photon Ultra's; the Mono's High Clear reads "1/").
- The Photon P1's fixed 0.45 s per layer (revision 2) is its firmware's frame time, found
  in Photon Workshop's own estimates (TD-122).
- Panel orientation follows each manufacturer's slicer, stored image and declared
  mirroring both, measured on a test model exported by ELEGOO SatelLite 1.0.2.43 and
  Anycubic Photon Workshop 4.2.1.20 (TD-153; the Photon P1 by TD-104, the Saturn 3 by
  ELEGOO's test file, TD-143). The Saturn 3 Ultra and Mars 4 Max mirror in X and Y; the
  Mars 4, Mars 4 Ultra and Saturn 4 Ultra 16K no longer do (revision 3). The Photon,
  Photon S, Photon Zero, Photon Mono and Photon Mono SQ have upright panels, the short side
  as the image width and the plate's X (revision 2), as Photon Workshop's files.

**Known limits:**

- The Photon P1's area (223.6416 × 125.976 mm) is Photon Workshop's; the manual gives
  223 × 126 mm and no pitch, and 5120 pixels at the 24.8 µm other sources cite would be
  126.976 mm. Its Standard Resin setting is Photon Workshop's default; Anycubic's resin
  page (A8) has listed the P1 since 2026-11 with other values (2 s, bottom 20 s × 1 layer,
  anti-aliasing 4, one 5 mm lift at 3 mm/s), which the catalog uses for its other resins.
- The Photon, Photon S and Photon Mono 4K's official printable areas (115 × 65 and
  132 × 80 mm) are smaller than their panels; placement uses the panel's area.
- The M5 and M5s pitch along Y (24 µm) is inferred from the official 123 mm over 5120
  pixels; Anycubic publishes only 19 µm. Their .pm5 and .pm5s extensions are not yet
  confirmed by an official source.
- The Photon S page gives a 165 mm height whose inch value equals 155 mm; the catalog uses
  the official 165 mm.
- The printers added on 2026-11-10 (phase 1, from the official manuals; Anycubic's product
  pages could not be read that day) have not been tried on the printers themselves, and
  the format each reads comes from its official test file (A12) and karasu-slicer's format
  research; the Photon Zero's .pw0 has no official test file. Where a manual's printable
  area differs from the panel, placement uses the panel's area: Photon Zero 97 × 54 mm
  (panel 98.64 × 55.44), Photon Mono 130 × 80 (130.56 × 82.62), M3 Max 298 × 164
  (298.08 × 165.6), M3 Plus and Mono X 6K 197 × 122 (198.144 × 123.84), Mono 2
  143.36 × 89.1 (143.36 × 89.6).
- The Photon M3's manual gives no pixel size: 40 µm matches its 163 × 102 mm over
  4096 × 2560 pixels. The Mono X 6Ks's 34 µm comes from Anycubic's 195.84 × 122.4 mm
  (A21); its manual gives 195 × 122 mm. The M5s Pro uses the wiki's 16.8 × 24.8 µm; its
  manual's 223.78 × 126.38 mm matches neither axis exactly (the same panel as the P1).
- The M3 Plus and the Mono X 6K are one panel and one format (.pwmb). The settings are
  Anycubic's resin table (A8), which disagrees with some manuals (Mono bottom 45 s, M3 Max
  bottom 30 s and 2 s off, Mono X 6K 4 bottom layers, Mono 4 retract 6 mm/s, M3 Plus
  1.5 s); its D2 row equals its M3 Plus row, which may be a copy error.
- Not included: the Photon X and the Photon Mono SE, whose official specifications could
  not be read; the Photon S and other Photon Workshop printers keep their own entries.
- The ELEGOO printers added on 2026-11-10 (phase 2) come from the official manuals (E3);
  ELEGOO's product pages could not be read that day. None has been tried on the printer;
  their panel orientation is verified against SatelLite except the Jupiter 2's, for which
  SatelLite 1.0.2.43 writes GOO version 5.2, a layout karasu-slicer does not read or
  write (TD-153). GOO is
  stated for the Mars 4, Mars 4 Max, Mars 4 Ultra, Saturn 3, Saturn 3 Ultra, Saturn 4 Ultra
  and Jupiter 2 (official .goo test files or pages); for the Mars 5, Mars 5 Ultra, Saturn 4
  and Jupiter SE it is inferred from their manuals' slicer, ELEGOO SatelLite, which writes
  only GOO (E6), as for the Saturn 4 Ultra 16K. CTB is not offered: the printers' firmware
  acceptance of plain CTB is not established.
- The Jupiter 2's manual gives a lift and retract of 60 + 280 mm/min where ELEGOO's table
  gives 60 + 390; the table is used. ELEGOO's table is also on its wiki (E10), identical.
- Anycubic's product pages, read on 2026-11-11 only in Internet Archive captures, disagree
  with its resin page (A8) in many values, as do some manuals; A8 is used. The differences
  are listed in docs/research/RESIN-CATALOG-RESEARCH.md.
- Not included: the Mars 4 DLP (ELEGOO states 50 and 52 µm, and neither gives its
  132.8 × 74.7 mm) and the first Jupiter (no pixel size or GOO statement could be read).
- Not supported: UniFormation printers (GKtwo, GK3 Pro, GK3 Ultra), owner's decision of
  2026-11-10. Users report that they read only encrypted CTB (the GK3 Pro's own .jxs is
  encrypted CTB under another name) and refuse files that fail their integrity check;
  encrypted CTB is excluded, and karasu-slicer does not bypass encryption or signatures.
  These are community reports, not UniFormation documentation (UVtools issues 673 and
  846, discussion 1084). Their users can export prepared models (STL, 3MF) and slice them
  in UniFormation's slicer; support needs a published format or UniFormation's permission.
- The Prusa SL1 and SL1S stay out until a PrusaSlicer export of the same model verifies
  their portrait, mirrored panel (owner, 2026-11-10).
- Panel orientation from ELEGOO's own test files (TD-143, 2026-11-10): the ROOK.stl of
  ELEGOO's Mars 4, Mars 4 Ultra, Saturn 3 and Saturn 3 Ultra test archives, exported by
  karasu-slicer, matches ELEGOO's ROOK.goo turned 180 degrees for the first three (now
  mirrored in X and Y, revision 2) and as stored for the Saturn 3 Ultra; none was mirrored.
  The Mars 4 Max's official file (no model beside it) declares the image karasu-slicer
  writes for it; it stays unverified. The Mars 5, Mars 5 Ultra, Saturn 4, Saturn 4 Ultra,
  Jupiter 2 and Jupiter SE have no official test file to compare.
